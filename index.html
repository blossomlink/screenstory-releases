Record raw. Style later.

Auto zoom on clicks

Your clicks are logged while recording and become smooth zoom-in, hold, zoom-out animations. The focus glides between nearby clicks.

Aesthetic framing

Gradient background, adjustable padding, rounded corners, and a soft shadow — applied at export, tweakable any time.

Webcam bubble

A circular camera overlay in the corner of your choice, with mic audio synced to the screen via a shared clock.

Crash-safe capture

The screen streams straight to disk untouched. 30+ minute recordings keep flat memory use, and a crash loses nothing.

WYSIWYG editor

Scrub any frame, edit zoom keyframes on the timeline, then export 1080p, 4K, or original size as .mp4.

Re-edit any recording

Projects keep their raw materials, so you can reopen, restyle, and re-export past recordings whenever.

Up and running in a minute.

  1. Download and unzip, then drag ScreenStory.app to your Applications folder and open it.
  2. Grant Screen Recording when macOS asks (System Settings → Privacy & Security), then relaunch once — macOS requires it.
  3. Hit Start Recording. The app hides itself; stop from the menu bar or ⌘⇧S, style in the editor, export.
